MADE FOR SLOW MOMENTS✳PLAYED WITH QUICK FINGERS

A pinch of practice.

  1. Find your flower. Put two fingertips on opposite sides of one bloom.
  2. Pinch inward. Bring your fingertips together to pick it. Lift both fingers before your next flower.
  3. Grow your bouquet. Every flower is worth one point. Pick as many as you can in 20 seconds.

On a computer, click flowers to practice. Keyboard players can Tab to “Pick a flower” and press Space or Enter. The timer keeps running while this guide is open.
